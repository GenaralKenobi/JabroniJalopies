# Jabroni Jalopies Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 9, 2026

This policy explains how **Tailgate Labs** ("we") handles information in the **Jabroni Jalopies** app ("the app"), which fleets use to track vehicle checklists, defects, equipment and maintenance.

## Who controls fleet data

The app is used by organizations ("fleets"). A fleet's managers decide:
- who joins the fleet
- which vehicles each person can see
- how long records are kept, within the limits below

If you use the app as a member of a fleet, your fleet's managers can see the information described below. Questions about how your employer or fleet uses that information should go to them.

## What we collect

| Information | Examples | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Account details** | Email address and a password. The password is stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider. | To sign you in and to match you to invitations. |
| **Profile in each fleet** | Name and role (manager or driver), visible to the fleet. Email address and optional phone number, visible only to you and the fleet's managers. | So fleet members know who did what, and managers can reach them. |
| **Vehicle information** | Make, model, year, VIN, license plate, odometer readings, fluid and tire specifications, vehicle photo | To run the fleet's maintenance. |
| **Inspection records** | Completed checklists: who completed them, when (by the phone's clock and when they reached our servers), results, readings and notes | To record vehicle condition and safety checks. |
| **Defect reports and photos** | Description of a problem, who reported it, when, and optional photos | So problems get seen and repaired. |
| **Truck contents** | What each truck carries and where it's kept, with who last changed each item and when | So crews know what's on the truck and where to find it. |
| **Equipment and chain records** | Chainsaws and ropes on each truck, problems reported on them, rope length changes, and chain counts logged at the end of the day — with who logged them and when | To keep equipment safe and track chain use. |
| **Service records** | Service type, date, odometer, shop, notes, and costs (costs are visible to managers only) | Maintenance history and sign-off. |
| **Technical data** | Information our providers need to deliver the service, such as IP addresses in server logs | Security and reliability. |

**Inspection records show who did what and when.** If you use the app at work, your inspections and reports are visible to your fleet's managers.

## What we don't collect

- **No location tracking.** The app doesn't use GPS. When you attach a photo, the app re-processes it, which removes embedded location data.
- **No advertising, analytics or tracking.** We don't use advertising identifiers, analytics tools or third-party tracking. We don't sell or share personal information for advertising.
- **No contacts and no other photos.** The app only accesses photos you choose to attach.

## Who can see your information

- **Members of your fleet**, according to their role:
  - Managers can see all of the fleet's data.
  - Drivers can see the vehicles they have access to, those vehicles' defects and checklists, and fellow members' names and roles. Drivers can't see other members' email addresses or phone numbers, or service costs; only managers can.
- **Our service providers:** Google Firebase (Google Cloud) hosts the database, authentication and photo storage on our behalf, under Google's data processing terms. Google doesn't use this data to target ads.
- **Legal requirements:** we, or our providers, may disclose information if required by law.

Members of one fleet can never see another fleet's data. This is enforced by server-side security rules, not just hidden in the app.

## Where information is stored

- **Our servers:** data is stored in Google Cloud data centers in the **United States** (us-central1). Some authentication data may be processed in other locations by Google.
- **Your phone:** a copy of your fleet's data is kept on your device so the app works without a connection. It's protected by iOS data protection, and it's erased when you sign out. Photos waiting to upload are kept on the device until they upload.

## How long we keep it

- Inspection records, closed defect reports and their photos, service records, chain logs and rope length changes are **deleted automatically five years** after they're created. Defects are deleted five years after they're closed.
- Managers can delete records earlier.
- **When you leave a fleet or delete your account:**
  - Your login, your profile, and your membership in each fleet are deleted.
  - Inspections and reports you submitted stay in the fleet's records, labelled with your name, until they're deleted under the schedule above. They're part of the fleet's vehicle history.
  - If you were the only member of a fleet, all of that fleet's data is deleted.

## Your choices and rights

- **Edit your profile** in each fleet under Settings.
- **Delete your account** in the app under Settings → Delete Account.
- **Ask for access, correction or deletion** by contacting us at **[CONTACT EMAIL]**. We may need to involve your fleet's managers for fleet records. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights:
  - **EU/UK (GDPR):** you can object, restrict processing, port your data, and complain to a supervisory authority.
  - **California (CCPA/CPRA):** you can know what we collect, delete it, correct it, and not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We don't sell or share personal information.

## Security

- Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our hosting provider.
- Access is controlled by per-fleet, per-role security rules on the server.
- No system is perfectly secure. If your phone is lost, ask a fleet manager to remove you from the fleet, and change your password.

## Children

The app is intended for people operating vehicle fleets. It isn't directed to children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above. For significant changes, we'll also let you know in the app.
