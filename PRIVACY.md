# Privacy Policy

The privacy policy now lives at [docs/privacy.md](docs/privacy.md), which GitHub Pages publishes as the public page the App Store links to.

Before publishing:
- Replace `[CONTACT EMAIL]` with your contact address, for example an iCloud Hide My Email address.
- Have it reviewed if you operate somewhere with special rules (EU/UK, California, government or healthcare work).

This is a starting point, not legal advice.
